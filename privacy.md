# BlueYonder Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 16 October 2026*

BlueYonder is a TV guide and player for Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by **Crow & Hawk
Technologies**, the trading name of James Crowther, in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use it. We're responsible for the app under UK
data protection law (UK GDPR).

## The short version

**BlueYonder doesn't collect any personal information.** There's no BlueYonder account, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, so we hold no personal data about you.

## What stays on your device

To work, BlueYonder saves the following **on your device only**:

- the sign-in details for your TV provider (kept securely in your device's Keychain)
- your profiles, favourite channels, recently watched channels and Continue Watching
- your settings, such as your Their Way line-up

We can't see any of this. Removing a profile in Settings deletes its details, and deleting the
app removes everything it has stored.

## Services the app connects to

BlueYonder doesn't provide any channels, films or programmes itself. It connects to:

- **Your TV provider**: the server or playlist address you enter, to load and play your
  channels, films and box sets. Your provider's own privacy policy applies to the information
  they receive.
- **TV listings**: free, publicly available UK listings, downloaded from GitHub
  (raw.githubusercontent.com) and epgshare01.online.
- **Channel logos**: the open-source tv-logos collection on GitHub.
- **Film and TV information**: The Movie Database (TMDB), for film and box set details and
  artwork. The app asks TMDB about film and programme titles, never about you.
- **Apple**: Apple's public film and TV charts, and links that open titles in Apple's TV app.

Like any internet connection, these services can see technical details such as your device's
IP address when the app requests information from them. BlueYonder doesn't send them your name,
your sign-in details (other than to your own provider), or anything about what you watch.

## Children

BlueYonder isn't intended for children. Adult categories are hidden unless you choose to show
them in Settings.
