# BlueYonder Support

BlueYonder is a TV guide and player for your own IPTV service, on Apple TV, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Amazon Fire TV,
from Crow & Hawk Technologies.

## Getting started

BlueYonder doesn't include any channels. You'll need the details from your own TV provider:

- **Xtream Codes**: the server address, username and password your provider gave you, or
- **M3U playlist**: the playlist link your provider gave you, and optionally a guide (XMLTV)
  link.

Open BlueYonder, choose **Sign in with Xtream Codes** or **Add an M3U Playlist**, and enter the
details.

## Common questions

**A channel won't play.**
Try another copy of the channel if one is offered, or check the channel works with your
provider. Some streams use formats Apple TV can't play live; BlueYonder tries other copies of
the same channel automatically.

**The TV Guide has no listings for some channels.**
Listings come from your provider and from free UK TV listings. Channels that neither covers show
without programme information.

**What is Their Way?**
In **Settings → Their Way**, you can arrange your provider's channels in the familiar UK channel
order of TV Aerial, Internet TV or Satellite TV services. It only renumbers channels your
provider already has.

**How do I remove my details?**
Go to **Settings → Remove this profile**. This deletes the profile's sign-in details,
favourites and watch history from the device.
